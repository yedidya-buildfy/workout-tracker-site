Workout Tracker

A data-first workout and health log: your lifts, food and daily habits as plain numbers — kept on your phone.

Coming soon to the App Store

Free · For iPhone · No account · Works offline

  • Log a workout as you lift

    Your last sets and notes are filled in. Tap to finish a set, and a rest timer counts up and changes colour when it’s time to go again.

  • Training blocks with goals

    Plan workouts on a calendar, group weeks into blocks, and set goals to work toward.

  • The small daily things

    Water, coffee, supplements, sleep and body weight, each one quick tap away. Steps, sleep and weight can come from Apple Health.

  • Nutrition without the fuss

    Search a food, scan a barcode or read a nutrition label. Values come from Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central; the targets are yours.

  • 3D muscle map and history

    Every exercise keeps its own history and records, and a 3D muscle map shows what you’ve trained.

  • Everything stays on your phone

    No account, no ads, no tracking. Nothing is sent to us, because there is no server. Export a backup whenever you like.
